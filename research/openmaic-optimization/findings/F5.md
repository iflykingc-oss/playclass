# F5: AI 生成课堂的教学质量研究（苏格拉底式辅导、建构式/探究式、多智能体教学、学习科学约束、幻觉与教育风险）

## Findings

### [1] 无护栏的生成式 AI 辅导会伤害迁移学习；同模型改为“扣住答案/苏格拉底式”后伤害消失，精修版 tutor 可显著提升学习
- quote: "an unguarded AI helper left high-school students about 17% worse on an unaided exam than peers with no tool at all, while the same model rebuilt to withhold answers erased the harm, and a well-engineered tutor roughly doubled learning"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26181
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-06-24
- confidence: high

### [2] 苏格拉底式引导（Socratic Guidance）相对提示词优化（Prompt Refinement）在后续学习增益与“理解驱动型提问”迁移上更优，尽管学生主观觉得更“低效”
- quote: "SG-students, relative to PR-student, achieved higher learning gains in later sessions, and were more likely to adopt understanding-driven prompting strategies, which are predictive of higher understanding, when using an unconstrained LLM. Although learners perceived the SG tutor as less efficient..."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03303
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-07-03 (AIED 2026 Best Paper; Springer LNCS pp. 546-561)
- confidence: high

### [3] 规模本身不会产生苏格拉底式教学行为；需专门对齐训练（启发式 RL）才能抑制“直接泄题”
- quote: "the best GRPO variant improves SE from 30.0% to 63.3% and lowers keyword leakage from 30.0% to 13.3%... an unaligned Qwen-72B baseline reaches 0% SE and 96.7% leakage, showing that scale alone does not induce Socratic behavior."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22996
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-07-24
- confidence: high

### [4] 苏格拉底式 tutor 存在“脚手架坍塌”：学生持续施压后模型逐渐放弃引导并直接给解；表征对齐可降低坍塌率
- quote: "under sustained student pressure, a tutor gradually abandons guided inquiry and reveals solutions directly... our method lowers Collapse Rate to 32%, delays average collapse onset beyond nine turns, and keeps over-refusal low"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19371
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06/07
- confidence: medium

### [5] 多智能体课堂架构（教学/备课/评估/学习者画像分工）在真实课程中可扩展，并能暴露教师看不见的课程缺口
- quote: "ITAS... Spoke-and-Wheel teaching architecture with quantum-specialized agents... Piloted in a quantum computing course at Old Dominion University... the analytics agent surfaces curriculum gaps the instructor could not otherwise see."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24807
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-04-27
- confidence: medium

### [6] 清华 MAIC（Massive AI-empowered Course）用 LLM 多智能体构建“AI 增强课堂”，在可扩展性与个性化之间折中；500+ 学生、10 万+ 学习记录的初步实证
- quote: "we propose MAIC (Massive AI-empowered Course)... leverages LLM-driven multi-agent systems to construct an AI-augmented classroom, balancing scalability with adaptivity... preliminary experiments at Tsinghua University... over 100,000 learning records of more than 500 students"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.03512
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-09-05
- confidence: high

### [7] 教师向多智能体（learner agent + teacher agent + evaluator agent）可按认知+动机画像生成差异化练习材料，八年级数学材料与画像对齐度高
- quote: "FACET framework... three specialized agents: (1) learner agents that simulate diverse profiles... (2) a teacher agent that adapts instructional content according to didactical principles, and (3) an evaluator agent that provides automated quality assurance... high stability and alignment between generated materials and learner profiles"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11401
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-08-15 (rev. 2026-05)
- confidence: medium

### [8] 学习科学要求 AI 课件具备理论根基：证据中心设计（ECD）+ 社会认知理论 + 最近发展区（ZPD）驱动的自适应脚手架与形成性反馈
- quote: "current LLM systems used in classrooms often lack the solid theoretical foundations found in earlier intelligent tutoring systems... we propose a framework that combines Evidence-Centered Design with Social Cognitive Theory and Zone of Proximal Development for adaptive scaffolding in LLM-based agents... Inquizzitor, an LLM-based formative assessment agent"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01503
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-08-02 (AAAI 2026 main track)
- confidence: high

### [9] Google LearnLM 把教学行为建模为“教学指令跟随”（pedagogical instruction following），专家偏好显著优于 GPT-4o / Claude 3.5 / Gemini 1.5 Pro
- quote: "we reframe the challenge of injecting pedagogical behavior as one of pedagogical instruction following... experts substantially prefer across a diverse set of learning scenarios, with average preference strengths of +31% over GPT-4o, +11% over Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and +13% over the Gemini 1.5 Pro model"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16429
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-12-21 (v3 2025-08-22)
- confidence: high

### [10] LLM 难度估计有系统性偏差：近似“课程序列难度”而非“误解驱动的认知难度”（Easy Trap），依赖其出题/自适应会引入评估偏差
- quote: "LLMs approximate curricular difficulty... rather than cognitive difficulty driven by learner misconceptions. This leads to systematic underestimation of misconception-driven items, a phenomenon we term the Easy Trap... an item with only 34.16% correct for 100 : 1/2"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26067
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-22 (EDM 2026)
- confidence: high

### [11] 幻觉与教育风险可通过“课程接地 RAG + 论断级验证 + 教学控制”显著压低；生成反馈需自动质检（DeanLLM 16 维，含幻觉维度）
- quote: "EduGuard attains the best correctness (90.1%), grounding (89.4%), and rubric alignment (90.8%), with the lowest hallucination (4.9%) and direct-answer leakage (9.8%). In the pilot, it raises immediate post-test accuracy from 68.4% to 81.2% and cuts overreliance from 38.0% to 17.0%"; "DeanLLM... 16-dimension evaluation framework covering feedback content, educational effectiveness, and hallucination risks"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15738 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05952
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-07-17 / 2025-08-07
- confidence: high

### [12] 多轮对话显著放大教学性失败：SafeTutors 发现 pedagogical failures 从 17.7% 升至 77.8%；核心风险是“静默侵蚀学习”而非毒性内容
- quote: "the primary risk is not toxic content but the quiet erosion of learning through answer over-disclosure, misconception reinforcement, and the abdication of scaffolding... multi-turn dialogue worsens behavior, with pedagogical failures rising from 17.7% to 77.8%"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17373
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-03-18 (EMNLP 2026)
- confidence: high

### [13] 建构式苏格拉底 AI（结构化对话脚手架）显著增强批判/独立/反思思维；“orchestrated multi-agent”作为教育者策展的专用智能体星座是后续路径
- quote: "Students using the Socratic Tutor reported significantly greater support for critical, independent, and reflective thinking... we introduce the notion of orchestrated MAS, modular, pedagogically aligned agent constellations, curated by educators"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05116
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-08-07
- confidence: high

### [14] 学习科学的位置规则：若 AI 让任务“变得毫不费力”，说明放错了位置；应保护首次艰难尝试与最终无辅助检查，中间用受护栏 AI 脚手架
- quote: "Secure the first hard attempt and the final unaided check, scaffold with guarded AI in between... if letting AI in makes the task feel effortless, it is in the wrong place."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26181
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-24
- confidence: medium

## Dead ends
- PNAS 原文（Bastani et al. *Generative AI Without Guardrails Can Harm Learning*）被 403 拦截；SSRN/CEPR 不可达。17% 伤害与“守卫版消除伤害”数字来自 Effortless Trap 对该 RCT 的综述引用，未读到一手 effect size 表。
- Semantic Scholar API 429；Bing 中国学术 SERP 质量差（产品页/词典），不可作证据。
- 含 OR 的 arXiv 查询返回 400/429，未能覆盖“worked examples / cognitive load theory”更细的实验效应量文献。
- UNESCO Guidance（2023，2026-01 更新）仅确认为机构级政策框架（人本、年龄门槛、伦理验证与教学设计流程），近 18 个月量化效果数据缺失。

## Suggested follow-ups
1. 拉取 Bastani et al. 一手论文（PNAS 2025 / SSRN）核对 17%、GPT Base vs GPT Tutor 的精确 effect size 与样本（~1000 高中生数学）。
2. 专题补搜“worked examples + LLM”“cognitive load + generative courseware”“spaced practice / formative assessment AI”的实验研究，补学习科学约束的量化证据。
3. 跟踪 OpenMAIC 同类：MAIC 清华后续发布、FACET 真实课堂 RCT、SafeTutors 11 维风险 taxonomy 直接映射到产品质检清单。
4. 评估“多智能体互动课堂”引入 evaluator agent（DeanLLM 式 16 维）+ answer-withholding 合同（per-turn help ladder）作为默认护栏。
5. 信任维度：Qadir & Mumtaz（IEEE EDUCON 2026）指出新手 automation bias / 专家 algorithm aversion，产品上应对新手默认更强脚手架、对教师开放覆盖权。
